# How low-rank is FastH3's backbone change, from bf16 (2026-09-29)

The fasth3-adapter direction asked: does a modest LoRA rank hold FastH3's
change to fl2va's backbone? Int8 could not answer it
(`2026-09-26_fasth3_lora_rank.json`), so this measures
`W_fasth3 - W_fl2va` on the bf16 sources
(`2026-09-26_fasth3_weights.md`, finding 1). CPU only. Tool:
`bench/measure_bf16_delta_rank.py`. Record:
`2026-09-29_fasth3_bf16_rank.json` (36 modules: blocks 0, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 37,
43, 49, each qkv, out, fc1, fc2; median and median-by-kind inside; an iid-noise
control at block 24 in `controls`).

## Reading

1. **No modest rank holds in the middle of the network.** At blocks 6 to 30
   the top 64 singular directions hold a small share of the energy for every
   kind, and 90% of it needs roughly half to three quarters of the full rank
   (`rank@90_share`). A LoRA that reproduced these modules would not be low
   rank.
2. **The middle blocks sit close to the noise control.** The block-24 modules'
   spectra are only slightly more concentrated than an iid matrix of the same
   norm and shape (`controls`). Most of that delta is not structure.
3. **The delta is at bf16's resolution.** `rel_delta` is about 1e-4 of the
   weight norm and `frac_changed` says only a few percent of elements differ at
   all. A change that small is quantised to one bf16 step on the elements that
   moved, and that rounding is itself noise with a flat spectrum. So the
   spectrum here is an upper bound on the structure in the true float delta,
   which no file we have holds. Every reading above is conservative in one
   direction only: the true delta could be more concentrated than bf16 shows.
4. **The ends are the exception.** Blocks 0, 43 and 49 concentrate more,
   `blocks.49.mlp.fc2` and `blocks.49.attn.qkv_proj` most (their `rel_delta` and
   `frac_changed` are also the largest sampled). That fits
   `2026-09-26_fasth3_weights.md` finding 5 (FastH3 and FlashGen agree in
   blocks 30-49). It is the only place a low-rank piece could earn its keep.
5. **Kind matters a little.** `attn.out_proj` is the most concentrated kind and
   `mlp.fc1` the least (`median_by_kind`).

## What it decides

Step 3 of the handoff was conditional on a modest rank holding. It does not
hold across depth, so the general backbone adapter is **not built**. Two
cheaper routes remain, and neither needs an extraction:

- **fl2va plus FastH3's gates only** (#35's `fl2va + gates` arm,
  `bench/fasth3_gates_arms.json`). If the look lives in the gates, as
  `2026-09-27_fasth3_swap.md` suggests, the transplant needs no backbone
  delta, and the gates are copied exact, not approximated.
- **An end-blocks-only adapter** (blocks 0 and 43-49) if the gates arm does
  not carry the look. It would be a small piece, but it is built on the
  weakest evidence here (three sampled blocks, bf16 noise floor),
  so it waits on the gates arm.

Not measured: blocks between the sampled ones (run with `--blocks` to fill in), the float
delta before bf16 rounding (FastVideo's unpruned V2 release would give it),
and whether a rank that holds little energy still holds the part that
matters to a render (an energy share is not a quality measure).
