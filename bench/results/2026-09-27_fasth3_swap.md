## What this retracts

`2026-09-26_fasth3_weights.md` read the time embedder as "where FastH3 lives"
because the modulation moves about 5% there while the backbone linears move
about 1e-4. The numbers stand; the inference does not. A 5% change to the
conditioning is functionally near-inert here, while the gates and the small
backbone drift carry the few-step ability and the look. Relative weight
change does not measure function. The records built on the inference are
deprioritised, not deleted (the owner's call):
- the dial files `..._temb_a05` and `_a075`;
- the PDD-on-FastH3-time-embedder sidecar.
