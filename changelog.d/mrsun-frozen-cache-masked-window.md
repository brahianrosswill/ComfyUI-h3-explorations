### Added

- `MiniMaxH3FrozenVideoCache` takes a masked video-to-video window, not only the audio-refine pass. The live rows are read per row from the masks core hands the model (text, every video row core regenerates, every audio row left open); everything else is kept from the build. A new optional `halo` input recomputes the kept video tokens within that many tokens of a regenerated one on every cached step, without regenerating them; its rows count toward the share limit, so a wide halo can switch the cache off for a window. A call runs stock, with the reason on its record, when it freezes nothing, regenerates nothing, carries a mask that is not on the layout's grid, runs under a context window, or has more live rows than `frozen_video_cache.LIVE_SHARE_LIMIT` of the sequence. The limit is reasoned from records of other passes and is not measured in this lane. The node is in no shipped masked graph.
- A slot now remembers a sample of what its kept rows were made from (the video latent under the kept tokens and every conditioning latent) and rebuilds when a call's differs, so another window or reference under the same shape is not served the first one's rows.
- `verify` on a masked window grades the video velocity on the regenerated rows, the rows next to a kept one apart from the rest, and runs the step once more with the text cached to say what live text buys.

### Changed

- Two things the audio-refine pass does differently, both by the same rule as a masked window: a refine call whose text and audio rows are more than the share limit of its sequence runs stock, and an audio mask frozen on some rows only now keeps those rows from the build where it recomputed them before.
- `bench/check_frozen_video_cache.py` items 9 to 16 hold the masked window on the CPU model, on a layout with a reference image's rows, tagged text and a token grid of three different sides: the live rows by name, each at core's own timestep row, a cached step equal to stock when nothing moved and visibly off when the region moves, a wrong live set further off than the right one, a new region or new kept content rebuilding, an oversized region, a misfit mask and a context window running stock, a partly open audio mask, the halo's rows by name, every row live being exact, and `verify`'s video figures against counts made cell by cell. It does not grade whether a halo or live text helps a masked step: on that model both are at bf16's resolution. Item 5 no longer asserts that live text leaves the audio closer to stock than cached text, a margin that held by the seed; it now holds, on three seeds, that live rows after block 0 are exactly stock's and cached text is not. Nothing has run on the card.
