## What the mechanism is

- (code) A latent `noise_mask` on H3 is a per-token timestep, not an attention
  mask: `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py::mask_row_values`, and the clean latent is
  re-injected every step by `comfy/model_base.py::MiniMaxH3.scale_latent_inpaint`.
- (code, survey) The release trains t2va, fl2va and ref2va. Its edit is
  ref2va with the source as a reference block and no mask. Every spatial
  mask on a base checkpoint, in every implementation read, is this
  inference-time method. Nobody had shown a mask together with a reference
  still; that is what was built.
- (code, reproduced by mrblue on a stub model) A mask does not survive two
  samplers as wired. A node that restores the plate between them fixes it;
  mrblue is building it.

## What gets replaced is a choice on the node

(owner, relayed by mrhf: "it should be a choice on a node".)
`MiniMaxH3MaskedSource.replace`: `whole subject`, or `head and hair`. With
the second, the body, the clothes and every movement stay the source's own
pixels, so nothing about the action needs prompting. The part is found by
core's SAM 3 detector on the frames the subject is in and kept only where it
lies on the subject's own mask.

- (inferred, not yet seen) A head regenerated on a kept body turns with the
  body, because the kept tokens around it show which way the body faces.
  The failure to look for is a face on a turned body.
- (unverified) How clean SAM's "hair" is on the lead under the gels and from
  behind, and what the model paints where long hair lay on the sweatshirt.

## The margin steers where the new subject stands

- (seen, owner's annotated frames) On the band clip at twice the default
  margin the replaced lead stands further to one side, which uncovers a
  member the source hides behind the original, and the model invents him.
  (reported, owner) The invented member keeps one identity through the shot.
- (inferred; the owner: "it makes sense") Why: the only thing telling the model where the original stood is the shape of the hole, so a wider hole lets him stand somewhere else.
  The margin and the still both changed between the two renders first
  compared. (seen) `band_changed32_w1` changes the margin alone, and at the
  default margin he is back in the original's footprint with the hidden
  member still hidden: confirmed, on one seed.
- (reported, owner) On that pair the wider margin "preserved identity
  better". (inferred) A tight hole makes the new subject take the original's
  outline; a wide one lets him keep his own proportions.
- (measured) The baseline chain, no distill and no Sol, took about six to
  seven times as long for the window and, to the owner's eye, kept the
  reference's identity worse. It samples with `er_sde`, which adds noise at
  every step, so it is not a like-for-like test of the model (owner). Not
  pursued: the render time is not worth it.
- (inferred) The reference still is a selfie and says nothing about height or
  build. The subject's size comes from the hole and the scene, which is also
  why a head-and-hair hole gave a head that size.
- So `grow_pixels` is the control for how far the new subject may differ from
  the old one in place and outline, and its tooltip now says so.

## Version one, and what it is

(owner: "Lets make that the shipped workflow"; "looks pretty good"; "if good,
we can lock in and say success for v1".)

- (code) `workflows/h3_video_to_video_masked_song_pdd8_api.json`: a source
  video, `MiniMaxH3SubjectTrack` (mrhf's, `subject_track.py`) for who,
  `MiniMaxH3MaskedSource` for what happens to them, the PDD8 song chain with
  Sol-Attn, the source's audio frozen. One pick, no object indices, no core
  tracker. The settings are `h3_config.SUBJECT_TRACK` and
  `h3_config.MASKED_SOURCE`, each equal to its node's defaults.
- (reported, owner) Pack nodes because they "simplify generation
  significantly"; two nodes and not one so that a change to the margin or the
  composite never runs the tracker again.
- (measured) The tracker on the band segment, as fixed twice during the
  afternoon: core's detector returns one detection per phrase unless the
  phrase carries a count, so the first version compared the subject with
  nobody; with every person detected, the lead scored well clear of the best
  wrong person and the default was set between them from that run. The
  numbers are in mrhf's note and in `subject_track.py` beside the constant.
- (seen) Thirty-two seconds through the shipped graph: the lead replaced in
  the three shots he is in, no cutaway touched, the neighbours intact.
- (measured, rows) What was compared on one window and dropped: the baseline
  chain (several times slower, a sampler that adds noise each step, worse
  likeness to the owner's eye) and the distill with Sol's window closed
  (slower, and the reference's clothes lost). The owner: "still seems like
  our original distill/sol worked best".
- (unverified) The kept mask (`mask_store.py`, mrblue's) wrote on the first
  run. Whether it is read back after a restart was not tested: inside one
  server session core's cache skips the tracker by itself.
