## 1. The turn, read as a mechanism

What the two records establish
(`bench/results/2026-10-04_masked_v2v_band.md`, "The turn without a
prompt"; `bench/results/2026-10-04_masked_v2v_turn_soft_arms.md`):

- reported, seen by those sessions: a late start carries the original's
  pose and its look together from the first knot dropped. Softening the
  source inside the region (grey, blurred) did not separate them: the
  facing cue survives a light blur and is gone at a heavier one, at half
  contrast, and when colour is kept, and every arm that turned him also
  changed his clothes.
- code: the only channel those arms used is the target latent itself. A
  late start puts the source into the region once, at the knot's sigma;
  a fractional mask value puts it there on every step (a row at mask value
  m runs at `m * sigma` and each step's output is `m * model + (1 - m) *
  plate`, `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py::_forward`,
  `comfy/samplers.py::KSamplerX0Inpaint`). So a soft mask on the body is a
  stronger leak of the original than the late start, not a weaker one. It
  is not a route to the turn.
- inferred: what enters the target rows is the model's layout prior and its
  appearance prior at once, and on an eight-knot distill the first step
  commits both. There is no step at which the model has the pose and not
  yet the look. Any method that puts the source into the target rows will
  bring its look. The model has two other channels that separate "what
  moves" from "what it looks like": references, where `<Video N>` is the
  trained source of motion and an edit and `<Picture N>` of identity
  (`ref-en.txt` 2.3; `docs/h3_references.md`, "Transfer motion onto a
  different subject"); and the Fun control adapter, which the owner has
  closed for this lane. The prompt is the third, and the owner does not
  want it to carry movement.

## 2. Routes for the turn, in the order I would try them

**A. The source as a reference video, beside the still and the mask.**
This is mrpink's arm C from the exploration (`2026-10-04_mrpink.md`) and
nothing has rendered it: no row names a masked arm with a `<Video 1>`, and
mrhf confirms (by message, 2026-10-05) that it was listed as thought of and
not tried, ranked last on 2026-10-04 for the cost of the reference's rows. The
relationship is the trained one and its text is generic, the same for every
clip: identity from `<Picture 1>`, motion and timing from `<Video 1>`,
`attribute_transfer` on the video. That satisfies the owner's rule that
nothing specific is prompted.

- code: our chain takes a video reference (`MiniMaxH3AppendRefVideo`), and
  the song node encodes references per window. A reference video is cut
  from its first frame to the render's length
  (`reference_conditioning.py::_prepare_reference_video`), so a window that
  starts late in the clip needs its own slice of the source: a small change
  in the song node, the reference's frames sliced by the window's start.
- Three forms of the reference, cheapest evidence first: (A1) the source
  window as it is; (A2) the source with everything outside the tracked
  subject greyed out, which the Subject Track's mask already gives, so the
  reference carries his motion and nothing of the plate the kept rows
  already hold; (A3) the subject desaturated and blurred, mrhf's softening
  moved from the latent to the reference channel, where the model is
  trained to take motion and not a copy.
- Cost and the checkpoint question. A reference video pays rows on every
  step (`docs/h3_references.md`, "A reference video costs rows in two
  places"). The shipped chain is the PDD8 fl2va bake; references on fl2va
  are attested for stills (owner, 2026-09-14, `audio_freeze_song.py`
  docstring) and never for a video. The ref2va checkpoint is the trained
  home of `<Video N>`. The first pair is therefore the shipped chain and
  the ref2va base on the same window, seed and still, against
  `turn_control`.
- unverified: whether a reference that is the same clip at the same timing
  helps or confuses a model trained on references that are other clips.
  Same timing is the best case for the trained motion relationship if it
  works at all.

**B. A mannequin of the original's motion.** Core ships SAM 3D Body
(`comfy_extras/nodes_sam3d_body.py`): from the source frames it recovers a
body mesh and renders it as a shaded mesh, a silhouette, an OpenPose
skeleton or capsules (`SAM3DBody_Render.render_style`). No hair, no outfit,
no graphic on the chest: what carried the garment in the soft arms is absent
by construction. Two uses: (B1) the render, over the plate, as the `<Video
1>` of route A; (B2) the render as what the late start is given under the
mask, in place of the blur, with the same one-knot start. Weights are not on
disk (`Comfy-Org/sam-3d-body`; size and licence not checked this session,
no network). It is a reference or a fill, not a control adapter. inferred,
and untrained: the model has not seen a mannequin reference, so this is a
probe. SDPose and MediaPipe are in core too; a stick figure is further from
a person than a shaded mesh, so the mesh goes first.

**C. Describe the action automatically, per shot, from the source.** The
prompted turn works (`ref2va_masked_lead_turns_away`). The owner's objection
is writing it by hand per clip. A node that captions the tracked subject's
movement per shot and fills a `[Shot N]` line is one generic pipeline for
every clip, and by the owner's rule for SAM phrases the text it writes is
visible and editable. Qwen3-VL is on this box as the encoder; the Gemini
bridge exists for an offline draft. Honest cost: it is still prompting, and
the owner said the point of the band clip is not to need it. Keep it as the
fallback that always works, and as the reading pass that tells route A what
to expect.

**D. The late start's untried arms** (listed in the soft-arms record): a
start between the first two knots, more seeds, another clip. By section 1 I
expect the look to ride with the pose there too; worth running only as
controls beside A and B. One more from mrhf (reported, 2026-10-05, by
message; written down nowhere before, untried): give the facing cue and
the garment different starts by token. Only the head and hair tokens start
from the softened source at the late knot; the body's tokens start from the
model-space zero latent, so the garment is left free. Their nearest arm,
`turn_soft_head30_late1`, filled the body in pixels from the surroundings,
which still hands the model a pale region, and gave half a turn and a pale
top. It is a cheap arm because it changes only what the late start is
given per token, and it tests the inference in section 1 directly: if the
body's coarse shape is part of the facing cue on this clip, as mrhf reads
it, this arm loses the turn too.

Not worth a render for the turn: a soft mask band on the body (section 1);
head and hair (no working region on the turn shot, and the oversized head,
`masked_v2v.md`); the control adapter (closed by the owner, 2026-10-04; its
weights are not on disk either).

## 3. Beyond the turn: what the build can do now with small changes

The pieces: a person followed across cuts (`subject_track.py`), a region on
the token grid with a composite and a kept mask (`video_mask.py`,
`mask_store.py`), the song node's source path, a plate restorer for two
samplers (`plate_restore.py`, registered and unwired), the SAM 3.1
conversion, Sapiens2 segmentation and matting on disk with their classes in
the venv's transformers (section 4), and VOID's weights on disk with core's
nodes (`comfy_extras/nodes_void.py`). Each line below says what it buys and
what it needs.

1. **Reshoot one shot** (a temporal mask). The cut finder already gives shot
   bounds. A mask that is all ones over shot k's frames and zero elsewhere
   regenerates that shot whole, with the plate exact around it and the audio
   frozen; the same machinery, the Masked Source taking a shot number or a
   frame range in place of a tracker mask. vllm-omni's latent-mask doc lists
   the same cases (removal, inpaint, continuation, extension). Buys: fix one
   bad shot of a render, or re-block one shot by prompt, without touching
   the rest.
2. **Keep the person, replace the world.** `replace` gains "everything but
   the subject"; the composite inverts; the reference becomes a setting
   still. Lip sync is kept by construction, the face being source pixels.
   The edge is then the subject's own and a matte (Sapiens2 matting) earns
   its place here where it did not for a replaced subject
   (`2026-10-04_mrhf.md`, "Matting").
3. **A part menu, and a garment swap.** Sapiens2's segmentation labels
   upper and lower clothing, hair, face and neck, hands, feet and shoes
   (`coderef/sapiens2/docs/SEG.md`). A per-frame part map on the tracked
   subject's crop replaces the SAM phrase detection for parts, which cost
   minutes before sampling and misses frames (band record, `band_head_w1`).
   The vendor's own edit example is a garment `attribute_transfer`. The
   owner's open item "the scene's clothes on the new subject" is the same
   mask from the other side. The licence clause mrhf recorded is the
   owner's call.
4. **Redub: regenerate the mouth to a new vocal.** Sapiens2 labels lips,
   teeth and tongue; core's MediaPipe face mask has a lips region. Mask the
   original performer's mouth, freeze everything else, feed a different
   vocal take; the audio-freeze lane already showed lip sync on frozen
   audio. inferred: the hole is small and anchored by the kept face, the
   opposite of the oversized-head case.
5. **A plate with nobody in it, from VOID.** Both passes, the T5 encoder,
   the VAE and the RAFT file are in the Storage folder; core's nodes take
   them. The affected region can be the grown SAM mask on a coarse grid,
   which the paper says is in distribution (`2026-10-04_mrhf.md`, "VOID for
   the plate"). Buys three things at once: remove a person; fill where the
   old subject stood and the new one does not; the shadow. Low working
   resolution and windowed; one probe when the card is free.
6. **Two subjects from two stills.** Two picks (two Subject Track nodes, or
   one with several), a union mask, `<Picture 1>` and `<Picture 2>`; the
   reference chain already takes several stills.
7. **Correct one shot by hand, without a frontend widget.** Core's
   `SAM3_Detect` takes point prompts and boxes (`positive_coords`,
   `negative_coords`, `bboxes`). A `corrections` text on the Subject Track
   ("shot 3: x,y" or "shot 3: none") seeds that shot from a click or empties
   it. This is the fallback the car clip needs
   (`bench/results/2026-10-04_subject_track_three_clips.md`) and it is
   transparent in the owner's sense.
8. **Any subject, not only a person.** `subject_phrase` is already free
   text; the head rule is what ties the node to people. A non-person mode
   that matches on the whole mask and asks for no head makes it general: a
   car, a guitar, a dog.
9. **Speed.** The frozen-row cache for partly masked windows is already
   listed in `docs/wiki/next_steps.md`; most rows are frozen in every
   window rendered so far.

## 7. The reference path, read in full, and what it changes about route A

Read the same day at the owner's ask: `reference_conditioning.py`,
`reference_encode.py`, `reference_order.py`, `h3_encoder_loader.py`, core's
tokenizer (`comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py`) and packed layout
(`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py::PackedLayout`), and the changelog from 0.173.0
to 0.186.4. Labels as above.

- code: a reference video is resampled to 24 fps from the loader's rate, cut
  from frame zero to the render's length, snapped to 17n+5, fitted to core's
  768-short-edge canvas and never enlarged under `comfy`
  (`_compile_reference_records`); the VAE encodes the full-rate frames, and
  the encoder sees every twelfth frame with a timestamp. Its DiT block sits
  ahead of the target in time and is attended by every target row at every
  step, pinned, never denoised. Timing reaches the target through attention
  over content, not through shared positions.
- seen: the card is a 24 GB 4090 and the masked graph's window is 345
  frames at 1344x768 (`workflows/h3_video_to_video_masked_song_pdd8_api.json`,
  node 74). `docs/h3_references.md`, "It does not all fit", records a
  345-frame reference video beside a 345-frame target going out of memory on
  a card of this size. So route A's subject-only reference at the source's
  canvas does not fit as the window is sized.
- Corrections to section 2, route A:
  1. Crop the reference tight to the tracked subject and scale it down
     before the Append node; `comfy` never enlarges a video, so the pixel
     area is set upstream. Read the conditioner's preview, which prices the
     rows before sampling. A shorter window is the other lever.
  2. A cheaper first arm: an encoder-only video reference (no VAE copy), so
     the encoder sees the subject at two frames per second and the DiT gets
     no rows. This needs `use_vae` on `MiniMaxH3AppendRefVideo`, listed as
     not done in CHANGELOG 0.185.17; a few lines. It tests whether the
     facing cue reaches the target through the text segment alone.
  3. The per-window slice must come before the compiler's cut: the song
     node hands the compiler a record whose frames are already the window's
     span, with the window's start in the conditioning key
     (`audio_freeze_song.py`, the `cond_keys` loop). Slice after the
     loader's 24 fps conversion, which every generated graph already runs.
- For route B: a mannequin reference is attended as content, never aligned
  by position, so the mesh goes over the plate or over a timestamp-bearing
  clip the model can match to the scene; a figure on black is the weaker
  form.
- Still open on the reference path from the 2026-10-03 review (its C5): a
  non-24 fps video is resampled in full before the cut, a video already at
  size is resized anyway, a mono track is duplicated before the slice.

## 8. Route A rendered: the turn is a step-count matter on ref2va, and not fl2va's at all

Written the same afternoon from the arms in
`bench/results/2026-10-05_masked_v2v_motion_arms.md` (rows in the `.jsonl`
beside it; the sheets are under `internal/claude/2026-10-05_mryellow/`).
The arm is route A in its encoder-only form from section 7: the Masked
Source's `motion_reference = subject only` (0.190.7) at the default short
edge with `motion_vae` off, the generic male prompt plus one relationship
sentence on `<Video 1>`, judged on the turn shot against the generic-prompt
renders at the same seeds.

What the rows say, in the order they were learned:

1. On the shipped graph (fl2va, its PDD8 bake, eight steps) the reference
   changes nothing: no turn on either seed, and the look drift seen on the
   second seed is the seed's, since the control at that seed drifts the same
   way with no reference wired.
2. On the ref2va base at sixteen steps the same reference turns him with
   the group on both seeds, with the prompt's look kept. A same-clip
   reference of the subject does not confuse the model: the pose follows
   the reference and the look follows the prompt and still.
3. The fl2va base at sixteen steps ignores the reference as its bake does.
   So the checkpoint is the first variable: fl2va does not take an
   encoder-only video reference of a subject in this setting, ref2va does.
4. On ref2va the reference is lost at eight steps whether the PDD8 bake is
   on or not, and at six evaluations on the uneven knot list. The bake at
   eight and the base at eight even share the spurious hand gesture. So the
   second variable is the step count, not the distill.

What that means for the lane. The turn can be had today, from the subject's
own frames, with no mask or prompt change, on ref2va at twelve steps or more;
eight loses it, so the threshold is between the two on this seed. It cannot be had on fl2va by this
route at all, which puts mrhf's per-token late start (section 2, route 1b)
back as the fl2va-side candidate rather than a fallback. The cost of the
ref2va route is the step count's; the song-node seconds in the rows price
it against the shipped graph. The choice between them is the owner's and is
on the board.

What the arms do not say. One band clip, one turn, one window; the reads
are by eye on five frames; the base arms have one seed on the step-count
ladder (eight, twelve, sixteen). Whether a video reference carries subtler motion than a full turn,
or holds across windows, is untested.
