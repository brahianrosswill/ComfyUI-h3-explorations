### How core's tracker behaves

Read in ComfyUI core, `comfy/ldm/sam3/tracker.py` and
`comfy_extras/nodes_sam3.py` (code):

- `SAM3_VideoTrack.max_objects` defaults to 4 in the schema; 0 means
  `INTERNAL_MAX_OBJECTS`.
- Tracks are never retired. Once `total_valid_entries >= max_objects`,
  `run_det` is false and detection never runs again: no new objects, no
  reconditioning, no `keep_alive` updates. This is why tracking every person
  spends the cap on the first shots.
- Unmatched detections are admitted in score order up to the free slots.
- With text conditioning, a track that detections stop matching counts down
  (`keep_alive`), and at zero or below its mask is written as `NO_OBJ_SCORE`
  for that frame. A dip under `detection_threshold` therefore blanks the whole
  mask for a run of frames.
- A detection scoring 0.8 or more that overlaps a track by half replaces the
  tracked mask on that frame, so the outline can alternate between the
  tracker's shape and the detector's.
- With `initial_mask` and no conditioning there is no detector, no
  `keep_alive` and no replacement. The mask must be on frame 0 of the frames
  passed, so this route needs one call per shot.
- `SAM3_TrackToMask.object_indices` selects by slot. A slot is not an identity
  across a cut.

### Protecting the neighbours (design, sent to mrpink, not built)

`grow_pixels` reaches into the people standing against the lead.

- One optional mask on `MiniMaxH3MaskedSource`, `people`: every tracked person
  (`SAM3_TrackToMask` with `object_indices` empty). The node subtracts the
  lead itself.
- protect = people and not grow(lead, `feather_pixels`). The lead's own pixels
  and a feather-wide margin always win, so a replacement slightly wider than
  the original is not cut off.
- Tokens: drop a token from the regenerated set only if every pixel of it in
  every frame of its run is protect. A token holding both people stays
  regenerated.
- Composite: alpha = `pixel_alpha(tokens)` times (1 - blurred protect), which
  returns the neighbour's source pixels inside mixed tokens.
- Where the lead's long hair covered the person behind and the replacement has
  none, the model invents that strip of the neighbour and it meets real pixels
  at the protect edge.
- Checks it would need: an absent or empty `people` gives today's output
  exactly; a token with one lead pixel stays regenerated; alpha never exceeds
  the token alpha.

Whether it is needed is open. On the sheets the neighbours already look right.

## 4. The flicker in masked_v2v_generic_30s

The owner pointed at the 20 second mark, beside the black trousers. That is
the wide profile shot against the painted backdrop, frames 438 to 538; the
source is idontwannatalk.mp4.

- seen, on enlarged consecutive crops of frames 474 to 485: a grey smudge
  beside the new subject's trouser leg at knee height, inside the regenerated
  backdrop. It sits where the source singer's light striped trousers are and
  moves with his knee.
- seen: the source has no cast shadow on the backdrop there.
- measured, `cell_probe.py`, mean absolute render-minus-source per 32 px cell,
  rows y 512 to 607, frames 466 to 493: every cell from x 704 rightwards stays
  between 0.5 and 0.9, so the composite edge is fixed at x 704 and no token
  changes state. The cells x 640 to 703, regenerated backdrop, differ from the
  true backdrop by about 5 on most frames and by 1 to 2 on single frames,
  mostly the last frame of a four-frame run (470, 474, 482).
- measured, `patch_probe.py`, the patch x 640 to 672, y 500 to 600, frames 440
  to 537: mean absolute step into a frame by position in the run is 3.2 for
  the last frame and 2.19, 2.28, 2.28 for the others. Twenty-four runs; a lean,
  not proof.
- measured: away from the subject the composite equals the source (mean
  absolute difference under 1).

### Why (inferred, not proven)

- It is not the start of sampling. `h3_config.PDD8_SIGMAS` starts at 1.0 and
  the flow noise scaling is `sigma * noise + (1 - sigma) * latent`, so a
  masked token starts as pure noise (code).
- The window encodes the raw source: `z = vae.encode(src_pixels)` in
  `audio_freeze_song.py` (code). The video VAE is a 3D causal CNN encoder with
  a ViT3D decoder (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py`, its header).
- So a kept token beside the subject was encoded with him inside its
  receptive field, in space and in earlier frames. It is re-injected clean at
  every step (`MiniMaxH3.scale_latent_inpaint`), and the model and the decoder
  can paint a remnant of him into the regenerated tokens next to it.
  `grow_pixels` at its default is one token of margin.
- The original moves far more than the replacement in that shot, so there is
  a lot of swept backdrop for the remnant to show on.

### The fix to test

Fill the masked pixels, per frame, over the grown mask, before `vae.encode`.
Keep the composite as it is, so true source pixels still return outside the
token mask.

- Locked camera: a per-shot temporal median of the source frames is a clean
  plate.
- Moving camera: a cheap fill from the mask's surroundings. The filled pixels
  are regenerated anyway; the fill only has to keep the original's trace out
  of the kept tokens.

The test: the same window and seed with and without the fill, then
`cell_probe.py` on both, comparing the x 640 to 703 cells. If the haze stays,
the explanation is wrong and the model is inventing it.

## 5. Models beyond SAM 3.1

Looked up on 2026-10-04. Hub search returned HTTP 500 all session, so repos
were found by id and by web search; a candidate I did not know by name may be
missing. Sizes are the weight files from the Hub tree API that day.

### Effects outside the silhouette

No model with released weights was found that outputs an object-plus-effects
mask for video. The route is a clean plate from an effects-aware remover, then
threshold(|source - plate|) unioned with the SAM mask. The ROSE paper builds
its training difference masks that way, threshold 0.09 (paper).

- **A second SAM 3.1 concept, "shadow"**: free to try. inferred, not run.
- **VOID is in ComfyUI core**: `comfy_extras/nodes_void.py` defines
  `VOIDQuadmaskPreprocess`, `VOIDInpaintConditioning`, `VOIDWarpedNoise`,
  `VOIDWarpedNoiseSource`, `VOIDSampler` (code). Weights
  `Comfy-Org/void-model`, apache-2.0, ungated: pass 1 11.14 GB, T5-XXL 9.79 GB,
  CogVideoX VAE 0.43 GB; pass 2 (11.14 GB) optional (card). Upstream
  `netflix/void-model`: default 384x672, at most 197 frames, the notebook asks
  for 40 GB or more of VRAM (card). unverified: whether it removes a shadow
  that is not marked as an affected region in its quadmask.
- **ROSE**, `Kunbyte/ROSE`: apache-2.0, ungated, a 3.13 GB transformer on
  `alibaba-pai/Wan2.1-Fun-1.3B-InP`. Built for shadows, reflections, light,
  translucency and mirrors; takes an object-only mask plus the unmasked video;
  480x720, frame count 16n+1 (card, paper). No ComfyUI node found.
- Ruled down: `jixin0101/ObjectClear` (an SDXL image model, per frame);
  `zibojia/minimax-remover` (cc-by-nc-4.0, no shadow claim on its card,
  unrelated to MiniMax H3 despite the name).

### Matting

Worth less than it looks (inferred): a matte describes the original's hair,
and the composite places a different subject.

- `SammyLim/VideoMaMa`: takes a mask on every frame (paper); 6.10 GB UNet on
  SVD-XT; weights under the Stability AI Community License, code CC BY-NC 4.0.
- `PeiqingYang/MatAnyone2`: 0.14 GB, NTU S-Lab License 1.0, human-only, takes
  a first-frame mask, so it needs reseeding at each cut.
- `hustvl/vitmatte-base-composition-1k`: apache-2.0;
  `VitMatteForImageMatting` imports in this venv. Per frame, trimap from the
  SAM mask.

### Instruments

- **PE-AV is retrieval-only for this purpose**: clip-level embeddings of
  audio, video and text; no sync task on the card. `pe-a-frame-*` is audio and
  text only, one embedding per 40 ms, returning time spans for a described
  sound (card). Both apache-2.0, ungated; `PeAudioVideoModel` and
  `PeAudioFrameLevelModel` import in this venv.
- No lip-sync scorer validated on singing was found. Usable ones:
  `ByteDance/LatentSync-1.6` carries `stable_syncnet.pt` (1.61 GB) and the
  classic `auxiliary/syncnet_v2.model`. Use it relatively: the source clip has
  real lips on the same audio, so score source and render per shot and compare
  (inferred).
- Zero-weight alternative (inferred): core's `MediaPipeFaceLandmarker` and
  `MediaPipeFaceMask` give a mouth-opening curve to correlate between render
  and source.
- Identity: `fal/AuraFace-v1` (apache-2.0, `glintr100.onnx` 0.26 GB; needs
  insightface or onnxruntime, neither installed here) or
  `minchul/cvlface_adaface_vit_base_webface4m` (0.46 GB, torch; MIT on GitHub,
  no license on the card). Record face size per shot.

### Sub-parts
