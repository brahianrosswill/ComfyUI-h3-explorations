The method

DMD, with one line changed

DMD trains a few-step student by pushing its outputs along the difference between two scores: an online critic trained on the student's own re-noised outputs, and the frozen teacher. PDMD keeps all of that. It removes the part of the update that points along r, the gap between the critic's and the student's clean-image estimates, which the paper argues is an unbiased estimate of the critic's own error.

d ← d − (⟨d, r⟩ / ‖r‖²) · r

Paper Sec 3.2, Eq. 4 and Algorithm 1. No GAN, no regression loss, no extra network or model pass. On H3 it is applied per sample, to the video and audio latents separately (App. C.2).

How the H3 students were trained

A LoRA on the base, from prompts alone

Teacher
Frozen H3, sampled without CFG because it is guidance-distilled (App. C.2; Table C1).
Student
A LoRA on every block's and token-refiner block's attention projections and both FFN layers. No adaLN, no proj_in or proj_out. The critic is a LoRA too.

VidProM prompts only, no real video (Table C1).

The sampling contract

ComfyUI's own schedule, exactly

The trainer runs diffusers' MiniMaxH3Scheduler: Euler with no re-noising, a grid of shift(linspace(1, 0, N+1)) at the base shifts, no guidance. ComfyUI's simple scheduler at shift 12 gives the same sigmas bit for bit at 4 and at 2 steps. So a PDMD graph is DISTILL_SAMPLING plus SIGMA_SHIFT and a step count. FlashGen is the one distill here that needs its own list.

Kijai's conversion

The right layout, a fixed share of each delta

Kijai fused q, k and v block-diagonally into qkv_proj, swapped the SwiGLU halves of fc1, folded alpha into B, then resized every module by SVD. The resize keeps the share of energy his sv_fro setting asks for and caps the rank at 128. bench/measure_pdmd_lora_conversion.py checks it against the published files, which we downloaded and matched against Hugging Face's checksums.

  • yesThe release's transformer/ equals FL2VA/transformer exactly and differs from Ref2VA/transformer.
  • yesq, k and v concatenated, and fc1 swapped, match the pruned fl2va checkpoint row for row at int8 noise.
  • noControl: fc1 unswapped matches neither the base weights nor kijai's delta, so the check can tell the two apart.
  • yesKijai's delta is a strict part of the published one: its norm ratio equals its cosine. What is dropped is the tail; nothing is rotated.

Loading on our checkpoint

Apply it at the call

On the int8 convrot checkpoint, LoraLoaderModelOnly merges a LoRA by dequantising, adding and requantising. A delta smaller than one int8 step survives only as rounding noise. PDMD's deltas are larger than FlashGen's but still under one step everywhere sampled, so PDMD needs MiniMaxH3LoRABranch, the loader h3_text_to_video_flashgen uses. Both it and the mutant pack's H3ExactLoRA parse both files as they are: every module finds its target, at scale 1.0. There's no adaLN in the LoRA, so the pruned checkpoint's adaLN basis trap does not come up.

Closest sibling

FlashGen

FlashGen matches PDMD on everything that decides how a distill is run and loaded. The real differences are the schedule, adaLN and the training canvas. FastH3 V2 shares the DMD name but differs on five axes. The paper's own nearest relative is its DMD† baseline, PDMD without the projection.

What does not line up

Four things to keep in mind

  • The released 2-step is not the one the paper scored. Table D2 reports an earlier iteration than the file's step 4000.
  • Critic updates per student update. The 2-step model card says 6:1, but the paper's H3 setup gives a different count. Perhaps the same schedule counted two ways; this is unverified.
  • Partition. The paper never names one. The README says FL2VA/T2VA, and the bit check confirms it.
  • Canvas and length. Trained and scored at 544p and 5 s. We render 1344x768 at 345 frames. The trainer's own example jobs use our canvas, but the paper's scores don't cover it. FlashGen has the same length gap.

Open work, in order

Rendered once, waiting on the blind scores

  1. The graphs: built, and rendered for the first look. h3_text_to_video_pdmd (4-step, full rank) ships at the root. The 2-step graph and kijai's two resize arms are probes in distill_experiments/. All run Euler on simple at 12/3 with MiniMaxH3LoRABranch at 1.0, and check_distill_settings.py grades them against the contract.
  2. Full-rank conversions: done. bench/convert_pdmd_lora.py writes minimax_h3_pdmd_{4,2}step_rank128_comfy. Every module's change is an exact copy of the published one, and both loaders place them at scale 1.0. They are what a PDMD graph will load; kijai's files become the comparison arm.
  3. A blind comparison against FlashGen: rendered and blinded, waiting on the owner's scores. Five scenes (three t2va, one ref2va, one i2va), each with full-rank PDMD, kijai's PDMD and FlashGen, all at Sol start_percent 0.2. It is judged on content-independent axes, because seed-matched clips from two distills are different scenes. Rows: bench/results/2026-10-01_pdmd_vs_flashgen.jsonl.
  4. Length. The trainer's 14 s harbour-cat job at our canvas is a ready first scene.
